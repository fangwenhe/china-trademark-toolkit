# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in improving the China Trademark Toolkit. This is a public-knowledge project aimed at helping foreign brand owners, cross-border sellers and overseas lawyers understand Chinese (CNIPA) trademark practice.

## What we welcome

- **Corrections** to outdated fees, timelines, forms or procedural descriptions (please cite the official CNIPA notice/source).
- **Translation improvements** (clearer English; accurate EN–中文 legal terminology).
- **New FAQ entries** for questions foreign filers actually ask.
- **Additional datasets** (e.g. expanded Chinese standard goods/services item wording by class).
- Links to **official, primary** sources only — no promotional or affiliate links.

## How to contribute

1. Open an issue describing the change and, where possible, the official source.
2. Fork the repository and create a branch.
3. Keep writing factual and neutral; this is general information, not legal advice.
4. Open a pull request and reference the issue.

## Style

- English-first for guides (a Chinese mirror may be added under a `zh/` path later).
- Use ISO dates (YYYY-MM-DD).
- Do not hard-code prices as permanent facts — always note "verify against the latest CNIPA notice".
- No personal data, no scraped private applicant information.

## Scope

This project covers **mainland China** national practice and the China phase of Madrid. It does not provide advice on US, EU or other foreign law, and it does not replace instructions from a qualified professional for a specific case.
