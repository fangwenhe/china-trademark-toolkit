# China Trademark — Official Fees & End-to-End Timeline

> Figures follow the current CNIPA/China Trademark Office schedule and are shown for planning only. **Always confirm the live fee schedule and examination periods with CNIPA before quoting a client.** Currency: CNY; USD is an approximate planning conversion (~7.1–7.2 CNY/USD).

## 1. Core official fees (national filing)

| Item | Official fee (online) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Filing, one class, first 10 goods/services items | **CNY 270 / class (~USD 37)** | Paper filing is higher (~CNY 300) |
| Each item beyond 10 in the same class | Extra per item | Keep specs tight to control cost |
| Issuance/registration | Included in the process | Electronic certificate standard |
| Renewal (per class, every 10 years) | See current schedule | Window: 12 months before expiry + 6-month grace (surcharge in grace) |
| Recordal of change / assignment / license | Per-recordal fee | Name/address change, transfer, license recordal |
| Opposition | Per-class fee | Filed within the 3-month publication window |
| Review of adjudication (refusal review 驳回复审) | Per-class fee | Request within **15 days** of refusal |
| Invalidation / non-use cancellation | Per-class fee | Dispute-stage proceedings |

> A foreign applicant additionally incurs **translation** and, depending on the situation, **notarization/legalization (apostille/consular)** costs and the **agent's service fee** — the official fee is only the floor.

## 2. End-to-end timeline (smooth case ≈ 6–9 months)

| Stage | Approx. timing | What happens / what you get |
|---|---|---|
| File online | Day 0 | Application submitted by the recorded Chinese agent |
| Acceptance notice (受理) | ~2–4 weeks (up to ~1 month) | Application number issued — **not** a registration |
| Formality examination | Weeks 1–6 | Corrections (补正) if documents/items are defective |
| Substantive examination | ~4–7 months | Distinctiveness + prior-right check; refusal may issue |
| Preliminary approval & publication | After examination passes | **3-month opposition window** begins |
| Registration certificate | After publication clears | 10-year term from registration date |
| **Total if smooth** | **≈ 6–9 months** | Faster in well-organized cases |

## 3. Dispute-stage timelines

| Proceeding | Deadline to initiate | Decision period (typical) |
|---|---|---|
| Refusal review (驳回复审) | Within **15 days** of refusal | ~9 months (+ up to 3 extension) |
| Opposition (异议) | Within **3-month** publication window | ~12 months (+ up to 6) |
| Invalidation (无效宣告) | After registration | ~9–12 months |
| Non-use cancellation (撤三) | Anytime after 3 years of non-use | ~9 months |

## 4. Madrid route timing

Home office → WIPO formal examination (weeks) → designation transmitted to CNIPA → China national phase runs on the same substantive standards; a **provisional refusal** must be answered in Chinese through a recorded Chinese agent within the stated term.

## 5. Planning checklist for quoting a foreign client

1. Count classes and items → official fee floor (CNY 270 × classes).
2. Add Chinese translation + any notarization/legalization.
3. Add agent service fee (retail vs foreign-associate wholesale).
4. State the **6–9 month** expected range in writing; never promise success or a fixed grant date.
5. Flag follow-on costs: renewal at year 10, monitoring, oppositions, platform recordals.
